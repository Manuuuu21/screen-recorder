How to Record (Offline)

1

Adjust Strategy

Choose "Audio Only" or "Video", and choose whether to capture tab audio, mic audio, or both.

2

Click Start & Choose Tab

A browser popup will launch. Go to the "Chrome Tab" or "Edge Tab" section and choose the Google Meet or video tab.

3

CHECK: Share Tab Audio

Tick the "Share tab audio" checkbox at the bottom left. If missed, participants will not be heard!

4

Stop & Save Clip

Click "Stop & Export". The app saves the full file locally, and you can also trim and download clips using the player.

💡 PERFORMANCE & SEEKING GUIDE

💡 What if I stay on Google Meet for hours? Will the recorder tab reload/discard?

Modern browsers (Chrome/Edge Memory Saver) automatically unload background tabs to save memory if left inactive. We have added 4 layers of protection:

•

1. Silent Audio Keep-Alive: Keeps media stream active so Chrome's Memory Saver treats this tab as actively streaming media (exempting it from sleeping).

•

2. Screen Wake Lock: Keeps your system and screen from entering sleep mode.

•

3. Continuous Disk Auto-Save: Recording chunks are auto-saved to IndexedDB disk storage every second. If your browser ever reloads, your recording is automatically recovered!

•

4. Navigation Lock: Prompts a warning before any tab reload or navigation occurs while recording.

⚙️

Chrome/Edge Settings Tip: For long meetings, open chrome://settings/performance and add this app's URL to "Always keep these sites active".
